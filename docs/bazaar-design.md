# Weekly bazaar and release 1.5

Release `1.5` groups the custom teams/rent mod with Visuality, Handcrafted, and Rechiseled. Minecraft remains 1.20.1; the user's wantlist links have 1.21.1 search filters, not authorization to migrate the world or choose incompatible artifacts. The original wantlist is preserved outside Git.

## Economy model

The rotating bazaar mints actual Numismatics currency when teams sell selected items. Team chunk-loading rent is a currency sink; ordinary player-to-player trading remains separate. All teams see the same active offers and the same unit prices during a week. Three shops are active by default, with the active count configurable to two or three. Prices, item allowances, and offered items rotate together. The item pool, selection count, rotation timing, price ranges, denominations, item quotas, messages, and activation are configurable.

Each pool entry exposes an item ID, minimum/maximum item count, minimum/maximum item price, and rarity weight. Item count means the week's maximum sale allowance PER TEAM for that item, counted as individual items rather than stacks or sale bundles. Price is the payout per individual item in the configured denomination. Roll both within their configured inclusive bounds when that item is selected. Higher positive weight means more likely to be selected; select distinct item IDs without replacement, not repeated retries that bias the pool. Reject negative/nonfinite weights and invalid ranges, and require enough enabled distinct entries for the configured shop count. Pool edits apply at the next rotation rather than rerolling an active week's quota or prices.

Initial candidate item identifiers are `minecraft:nether_star`, `minecraft:end_rod`, `minecraft:shulker_shell`, `minecraft:wither_skeleton_skull`, `minecraft:ancient_debris`, `minecraft:diamond`, `minecraft:emerald`, `minecraft:bone_block`, and `minecraft:iron_block`. Validate identifiers against the actual server registry; select without replacement. Price and quantity units must be explicit: item count, not stacks, with native Numismatics amounts represented as whole spurs internally. Do not activate invented economic rates silently; show an operator-ready configuration and require explicit activation.

Use a common real-time UTC week boundary, configurable independently from each team's prepaid rent anniversary. Generate and persist the current week's offers once, including selected item IDs, fixed unit prices, and quota definitions. A restart, configuration reload, or clock check must not reroll the active week's choices or reset its usage. Pool/range edits apply to the next rotation unless the administrator explicitly performs a logged override. After long downtime, generate the current period only; do not replay missed offers.

Default payouts go into the selling team's Numismatics treasury, from which rent is paid. A configurable alternative may pay the seller's personal account, but must still consume that team's allowance. Show the destination clearly before selling. Configuration and examples are not a claim that the live economy has been activated.

## Quotas and the accepted loophole

Each offer has a per-team weekly maximum item count. All members consume the same allowance for that team and offer. Team renames, owner transfers, reconnects, and server restarts do not reset it. Quota state and minted bank currency share the Numismatics bank persistence boundary used by the teams mod.

The user explicitly chose ONLY per-team quotas and accepted team-switching/recreation loopholes. Do not add a hidden per-player ceiling, membership lock, account-wide cap, or anti-hopping penalty. Creating another team can therefore gain that new team's allowance. This limitation must remain visible: per-team limits bound each team's minting, not necessarily each human's total minting.

## Physical access and GUI

The user will place three blocks, look at each one, and run an operator command to bind it to shop slot 1, 2, or 3. Use a bounded line-of-sight raycast to the block being looked at; no coordinate entry is required. Do not place or replace production blocks automatically. Each registered counter opens its corresponding weekly offer, not all three shops at one counter. The block positions persist by dimension and coordinates while their items/prices/allowances rotate. If the operator configures only two active offers, the third counter is visibly unavailable.

Players access the server-driven shop GUI by clicking these counters in the existing bazaar. Use supported existing vanilla blocks and a vanilla screen protocol, avoiding a required custom client Java mod. The badge resource pack remains independent. Binding must not destroy an existing inventory or turn an unlimited native Numismatics vendor into a merely hidden mint. Reject unsafe native economic blocks as counters. Operators can unbind/rebind a slot when moving the bazaar. Registered-counter clicks are handled even on permission/availability failure rather than falling through to an unrelated underlying block GUI.

Offer actions show unit price, sale amount, total payout, destination, week end, and remaining quota. Every click revalidates current week/offer identity, team membership, permissions, item count, quota, payout range, and destination before consuming anything. Reject stale menus after rotation rather than trading at an old price. Reject or safely handle shift-click, drag, number-key swaps, double-click collection, and client-supplied display-stack movement; visual offer icons are not transferable inventory.

Each counter also has a floating model of its current item above the block, with readable text for the unit price, reset countdown, and the observing player's OWN team's remaining allowance. This remaining amount must be personalized by viewer/team; one shared global text value is incorrect. Players without a team see an invitation to join a team instead of a sale allowance. Use native 1.20.1 item/text display protocols, not collectible dropped items or a client Java mod. Bound visibility to nearby viewers and refresh at a configurable, bounded interval rather than ticking a large population of world entities. Update after sales, membership changes, and weekly rotation; remove displays after unbinding, broken/replaced counters, world changes, or leaving range. Do not persist duplicate display entities across restarts. Presentation, height, range, refresh interval, and text are configurable. The real-client acceptance includes inspecting both the floating item and personalized text for two different team allowances.

Sales consume matching items from the player's ordinary inventory, not from nested shulkers or arbitrary remote inventories. Price changes are server-authoritative. Handle insufficient items, full quota, zero/negative counts, multiplication overflow, double clicks, concurrent members, disconnect, and reload without minting extra money or losing items on rejected operations. Player inventory files and bank saved data are separate persistence boundaries; do not claim hard-crash atomicity merely because balance and quota share one NBT root. A durable sale journal must either reconcile that boundary or identify ambiguous recovery and fail closed.

## Sale journal and recovery

Resolve the market period before admission, then freeze the transaction ID, player/team/destination account IDs, market generation, item/count, unit price/payout, and exact inventory-slot evidence. Validate destination balance headroom as well as the payout itself: Numismatics' native `deposit(int)` can overflow an existing balance. Permit at most one unresolved sale per player, and reserve its team quota so another member cannot oversubscribe the last allowance.

Persist and verify a PREPARED record in bank-attached state before removing items. Then consume the prevalidated items and attach a transaction-bound receipt to the same player NBT image as the inventory. Save and read back that receipt and the expected inventory representation before crediting the frozen destination. Finally persist and verify COMMITTED, quota usage, and native credit together before announcing success or making an uncertain credit spendable. Vanilla bank and player-save methods swallow errors, so their return is not proof. A count or hash without a transaction-bound player receipt is insufficient recovery evidence.

After interruption, PREPARED plus matching durable removal proof can resume its frozen commit exactly once or remain quarantined. PREPARED without trustworthy proof must be quarantined, not paid/refunded by guessing from current inventory. COMMITTED is never replayed; missing/conflicting player proof requires operator recovery. Keep pending records bound to their original generation and account through rotation, disbanding, and owner changes. Retain compact committed receipts until a separately verified compaction/checkpoint protocol makes removal safe; a visible bounded journal may reject new sales when full rather than delete proof unsafely. Disabling the bazaar blocks new sales but never erases unresolved evidence.

Rotation must not move generation IDs backward after a system-clock rollback. Retain the active persisted generation until its end. A manual override creates a new generation and invalidates old menus, without erasing prior obligations or repricing pending sales.

## Replacing the old mint

Read-only inspection found the original creative vendor in overworld `(279, 93, 112)` with Numismatics block-entity ID `numismatics:vendor`, Mode 1, one shulker shell as its selected item, and price 8 spurs. The inventory was empty. This was independently confirmed on the live server; the historical original backup agrees. The existing vendor belongs to xpcmc.

Do not mutate this block during development, tests, or automatic deployment. The user will place and bind the three new counter blocks themselves after the mod is complete. Before activating the capped economy, the old native unlimited mint must be retired as a separate, explicit operator action: intercepting only its click GUI is insufficient if its native vendor or automation can still mint. For any authorized retirement, take a consistent backup and revalidate the block identity, owner, price, item, and empty inventory immediately beforehand. Preserve its original NBT and backup for rollback. Do not leave an unlimited item or alternative minting route accessible by dropping the old creative vendor during replacement. Other player-owned shops are outside the migration scope.
